DARK VENGEANCE

In a kingdom consumed by betrayal, one warrior rises from the ashes to reclaim what was stolen. Choose your path, wield forbidden power, and carve your name into legend.

WHERE THE PATH FORGETS ITS NAME

The Whispering Grove

The trees here remember every traveler who passed beneath them — and few who wandered off the stones ever found their way back. At the grove's heart, a moonlit shrine waits behind broken arches.

Follow the pale light. It is either a guide, or a warning.

BEYOND THE BROKEN GATE

The Hollow Spire

Rain has not stopped falling on these grounds in a hundred years. What was once a seat of kings now stands hollow — its towers watching over ruins that even the crows have abandoned.

Cross the shattered stream and the old wards will wake. Few who enter the Spire return with their names intact.
